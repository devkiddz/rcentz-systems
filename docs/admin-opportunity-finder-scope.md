Finish live customer-workspace verification before completing the admin. This document records scope; it does not implement the finder.

One finder, two lanes: Jobs & Contracts, and Business Prospects. Initially private to Dennis; later business prospects may be shared with explicitly authorised staff. Personal employment searches must not become visible to other admins by default. Useful parts may later integrate with Job Rcentz.

Match against Dennis's actual skills, 2+ years of experience, portfolio, location, relocation/remote preferences and availability. Separate employment preferences from Rcentz service offerings. Preserve unknown salary, mentorship or country eligibility as unknown. Flag missing qualifications rather than inventing suitability.

Start with a few supported structured sources. Each connector runs on a schedule, preserves original URLs and dates, deduplicates results and checks whether applications remain open. Source access must be verified individually.

Explain matches, missing requirements, Nigeria eligibility and questions to clarify. For business prospects distinguish explicit buying/hiring intent from a business that may benefit; an outdated website alone is not evidence of demand.

Provide a concise daily digest with the strongest new opportunities and dashboard links. Do not fill quiet days with weak matches. Track New, Shortlisted, Applied/Contacted, Replied and Closed, plus feedback refining selection.

Prove Jobs & Contracts end to end first, then expand prospect discovery. Prepare tailored applications, scopes of work (SOWs) and proposals for review. Applications and outreach require human review before sending; no automatic unreviewed submissions.

Success means useful opportunities and replies, not raw listing volume. Planned integration: private admin pages, source/profile/opportunity/run records, scheduled collection and digest, existing notification infrastructure, optional AI-assisted interpretation grounded in the original listing.
